# Frida-in-the-Prison-Lab
A simple 2D platformer game I'm creating in Pico-8, following one of the MC's in my 5-book Sci-Fi LitRPG series "Overtaken Online" (published 2021-2024). 

In this game, we follow Frida Erikson in her first days stuck in the Daemonorg Prison-Lab, before her first meeting with Darius Hunter (which takes place in the series' companion novella "Impending Doom").

The Overtaken Online book series can easily be found on Amazon and Audible, or on my website: benormstad.com
